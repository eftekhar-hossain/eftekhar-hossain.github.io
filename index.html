Eftekhar Hossain

Bio: Hi, I'm Eftekhar, a 2nd-year CS PhD student at the University of Central Florida (UCF), USA. At UCF, I am a part of the Bridge-AI Lab. Previously, I did my MSc (2023) and BSc (2018) in Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering (ETE) from Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology (CUET), Bangladesh. I was a research member of CUET NLP Lab from 2021-2024, where I had the privilege to work with Prof. Dr. M. Moshiul Hoque and Omar Sharif.

🌲Research Interests: My primary research interests are Natural Language Understanding and Multimodal Learning, with a broader interest in the areas of Machine Learning and Generative AI. My current work specifically focuses on investigating the ability of large language models in Multi-Perspective Narrative Understanding to support better decision-making in urgent scenarios.

In my earlier research, I developed resources and computational models for Multilingual NLP with an emphasis on Multimodality in machine learning. My work focused on developing benchmarks and techniques for effectively integrating information from multiple modalities (e.g., image and text) and jointly evaluating them to solve complex language and vision problems in low-resource scenarios.
